Plan the request

Agreeing how the finished work will be checked and recorded

Without an agreement before work starts, a finished job may be judged only by whether it looks complete. If you are sending a request for your Alliston home, list what you would want the provider to check at the end, whether photos are expected of anything hidden before it is closed, and what the completion record should list. Parts, settings and checks the provider describes can then be compared with what was actually done.

  • Checks at the end

    Ask the provider to describe what will be checked on each item once the work is finished.

  • Photos of hidden work

    Ask whether photos will be taken of pipe, connections or fittings before a wall, floor or pit cover closes them in.

  • Completion record

    Ask for a written record listing parts installed, settings left in place and any separate items noticed during the work.

Before you send

What to Photograph Before Requesting a Quote

Photos of the right parts save a lot of back-and-forth. Take these before you send the request and have them ready for the follow-up.

  • Water heater: the rating plate, the vent pipe where it leaves the tank, and the rental sticker or contract if there is one.
  • Blocked drain: which fixtures back up, whether it only happens on the lowest floor, and the floor drain or cleanout cap if you can find it.
  • Leak: the wet area or stain, the water meter with all fixtures turned off, and any recent work near the leak.
  • Sump pump: the open pit and the pump inside it, the discharge pipe where it exits, and any backup pump or alarm.
  • Older supply pipe: the pipe beside the water meter. Copper looks orange-brown, galvanized steel is grey and threaded, and lead is dull grey and turns shiny silver where you scratch it with a key.
FAQ

Questions About Your Alliston Request

Once my request lists the end checks I want, has a plumber been found who covers my Alliston address?

No. Sending a request does no more than start a review by our enquiry team. Whether a provider can cover the address, what work is offered and how it is priced and scheduled are all settled between you and that provider, and nothing is booked until you agree to a written scope.

What should I ask for so the end of a job in my Alliston home is checked and recorded?

Ask the provider to list, before work starts, each check it will carry out when finished and what the written record will contain: parts, settings, photographs of anything concealed and separate items noticed. Put that list into the written scope so both sides can compare it afterwards.

Do I have to test the equipment myself when the plumbing work in Alliston is finished?

No. Every check belongs to the provider, who should perform it and describe the result. Your part is to ask what will be checked, read the completion record and raise it with the provider if a detail in it does not match what was agreed in the written scope.

Can one request cover several jobs such as a heater, a drain and a toilet?

Yes, list each job separately and say what you want checked and recorded for each. Items that are linked, such as a leak and a fixture above it, may suit one request; unrelated items are clearer as separate requests, each with its own completion record.

What photographs should I ask for before pipes or a sump pit are covered in Alliston?

Ask the provider to photograph any concealed work before it is closed, such as new pipe in an open wall, a drain connection or the pit and discharge line. Ask where the photographs will be kept and whether they will be listed in the completion record.

What if the provider finds something outside the agreed work at my Alliston property?

It should be written as a separate item with a description and, if possible, a photograph, not folded into the agreed job. You can then decide whether to ask for a new scope for it, and the original completion record stays focused on what was agreed.
